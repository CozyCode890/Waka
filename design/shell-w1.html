What this page cannot tell you

Mica is approximated by a CSS blur over a fake wallpaper. Real Mica tints the desktop wallpaper, not a gradient, and whether it runs on the fast painter at all is S00's first exit test.
Fonts render differently in JavaFX. Browser text shaping is not JavaFX's; expect to retune one or two sizes at S01.
Nothing here is fast or slow. The 100k-row and 2,000-attribute tests are S00 exit tests on real data, and no HTML page can stand in for them.
